Source notes

This web edition preserves the campaign supplied by Jeremy in The Jewel of Lustria – Expedition Campaign.

Scenario V

The source contains three versions. Following the author’s direction that earlier versions precede the final, this edition uses the last rules block: the six-turn race through a central Convergence Zone, with four initial defenses and three additional defenses per new branch. The identical narrative introduction is retained once. The superseded two-phase Gauntlet variants and their placeholder spell tables are not part of the playable reference.

The two track diagrams come from the original standalone 05.Scenario.5.Layouts.html, uploaded 11 April 2026, and illustrate the Convergence Zone layout.

Editorial treatment

Rules and narrative wording are preserved except for the author’s requested faction corrections: Ogre Kingdoms are grouped with the Eastern Kingdoms, Halflings with the Empire as inhabitants of the Mootland, and Albion with the Western Kingdoms. Page breaks, typography, navigation and table layout are adapted for the web. Scenario I and IV have different names in the narrative overview and detailed battle rules; both original wordings remain. No hex-map follow-up material is included.
